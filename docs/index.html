What's being integrated

Each step resolves gravity against a drag force opposing the projectile's velocity relative to the air, drag = ½ρCdA·v²/m. Air density itself falls off with altitude by the barometric formula, so a shot that climbs higher meets thinner air on the way up than the way down.

RK4 vs. Euler

Euler advances position and velocity with a single slope evaluation per step — fast, but it drifts on curved trajectories. RK4 samples the slope four times per step and blends them, staying accurate even as drag bends the path away from a parabola.
